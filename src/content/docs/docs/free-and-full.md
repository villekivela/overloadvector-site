**Overload Vector is free in the beta.** Everything in these docs is included. There is no paid
tier, no subscription and no card to enter.

A paid version through Google Play is planned for later. This page will say what stays free and what
the full app costs before anyone is charged. Ending a paid plan won't delete or hide anything you've
logged.

## What Claude costs

Coaching uses your own Claude key or account. Overload Vector doesn't bill for it.

- **With an API key**, Anthropic charges your Claude Console account for what each check-in uses.
  Settings shows an estimate for the month. Set a monthly spend limit in the Console.
- **With the connector**, your use counts against your own Claude plan.

You can use the app without either.
