---
title: Contact and support
description: How to reach Overload Vector's support.
---

Email **[support@overloadvector.com](mailto:support@overloadvector.com)** for help, bug reports,
ideas and privacy requests.

For a problem, it helps to include:

- your phone model and Android version
- the app's version, the last line of **Settings**
- what you did, what you expected and what happened, with a screenshot if you have one

Please don't send your Claude API key or a backup file unless support asks for one. A backup holds
all of your training.

You may find your answer in the [FAQ](/faq/) or the [docs](/docs/getting-started/).
