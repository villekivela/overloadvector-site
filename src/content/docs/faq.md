## Why does Overload Vector exist?

A training log records what was lifted. Overload Vector also runs the program. It suggests each
weight from the last session, and Claude can propose a change to the program when pain, time or a
missing machine gets in the way.

## Is my data private?

Your training stays on your phone. The app has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it needs no
account. It goes elsewhere only if you choose: to Anthropic when you check in with your own Claude
key, to your Overload Vector account if you connect Claude, and to your Google account if Android's
backup is on. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) has the details.

## Do I need a Claude account?

No. Logging, programs, progression, history and backups all work without one. For
[coaching](/docs/coaching/) you need either an Anthropic API key or a Claude account to use with the
connector.

## What does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Overload Vector is [free in the beta](/docs/free-and-full/), so there's nothing to cancel. What you
spend on Claude is between you and Anthropic: remove your key in **Settings → Claude key**, delete it
in the Claude Console, or change your Claude plan there.

## What happens when a subscription ends?

There are no subscriptions yet. When a paid version arrives, ending it won't delete or hide anything
you've logged.

## Which phones?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer and a 64-bit ARM processor. There is no iPhone or web
version. The app is in English, in kilograms or pounds.

## How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one. See
[Backups and moving to a new phone](/docs/backups/).

## How do I delete my data?

On the phone, uninstalling the app deletes it; Android's backup in your Google account is managed in
your Google settings, and backup files are yours to delete. If you connected Claude, tap
**Settings → Delete my account data** first, which deletes the copy on the server.

## How do I get help?

Email [support@overloadvector.com](mailto:support@overloadvector.com). Say your phone model, your
Android version and the app's version (the last line of Settings). See [Contact](/contact/).
