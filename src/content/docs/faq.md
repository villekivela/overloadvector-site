## Why does Overload Vector exist?

To run a strength program and log it in one app. Overload Vector suggests each weight from the last
session. When you can't train as planned, Claude can propose a Change, a workout or a new
program.

## Is my data private?

Your training stays on your phone. The app has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it
needs no account. Your training goes elsewhere only if you choose: to Anthropic when you check in
with your own Claude key, to your Overload Vector account if you connect Claude, and to your Google
account if Android's backup is on. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) has the details.

## Do I need a Claude account?

No. Logging, programs, progression, history and backups all work without one.
[Coaching](/docs/coaching/) needs an Anthropic API key, or a Claude account for the connector.

## What does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Overload Vector is [free in the beta](/docs/free-and-full/), so there's nothing to cancel. What you
spend on Claude is between you and Anthropic. To stop it, remove your key in **Settings → Claude
key**, delete it in the Claude Console, or change your Claude plan there.

## What happens when a subscription ends?

There are no subscriptions yet. When a paid version arrives, ending it won't delete or hide anything
you've logged.

## Which phones?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer and a 64-bit ARM processor. There is no iPhone or web
version. The app is in English, in kilograms or pounds.

## How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one. See
[Backups and moving to a new phone](/docs/backups/).

## How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone. Android's backup in your Google account is
managed in your Google settings, and backup files are yours to delete. If you connected Claude, tap
**Settings → Delete my account data** first, which deletes the copy on the server.

## How do I get help?

Email [support@overloadvector.com](mailto:support@overloadvector.com). Say your phone model, your
Android version and the app's version, which is the last line of Settings. See [Contact](/contact/).
